The rest of the week went the same as every week did when nothing got fixed: shifts logged, meals eaten standing at the counter, three separate excuses invented for why he still hadn't asked Kessler outright. Wednesday he almost did, twice, and both times found a reason to walk past her door instead. By Thursday morning the excuses had finally run out, the week having burned through every hour there was left to hide in. He found the register drawer unlocked when nobody else was in early, and the ledger Mair swore she'd returned two weeks ago was gone entirely from the shelf where it should have been sitting.

**Choices offered:**
1. Go straight to Kessler's office and report it missing.
2. Search the archive room himself before saying anything.
3. Find Mair first, and watch her face when he asks.
