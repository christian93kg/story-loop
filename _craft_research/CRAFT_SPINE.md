# Craft Spine

Always-on. The through-lines are the craft. The ship tests are the four checks the through-lines do not hand you a procedure for. Per-mode detail lives in `cards/`, worked examples in `reports/`.

## The through-lines

- **Withhold / omission** — cut the named feeling, the verdict, the explanation; the reader supplies more force than you can.
- **Causality, not chronology** — beats join on "therefore/but," never "and then"; each changes one fact.
- **Perception over stated emotion** — report what the body notices, not what it feels.
- **Narrator stays out** — no authorial scoring, meta-commentary, or "moving moment" certification; the shown detail forces the conclusion.
- **One telling particular over catalogue** — a single load-bearing detail beats piled adjectives or inventory; motion beats nouns wearing adjectives.
- **Restraint amplifies under pressure** — flatter and quieter as the screws tighten; volume down as wrongness rises.
- **Hand back on a want-at-risk** — end on the cusp of an outcome still in doubt, dead time compressed.
- **Voice through syntax, never spelling** — origin and stress ride word-order, articles, diction, rhythm; never eye-dialect.

## Before you ship

- Delete this sentence — does the next still make complete sense? If yes it advanced nothing.
- Put "therefore" or "but" between consecutive beats; if only "and then" fits, rebuild the link.
- Name a different downstream state for each choice option; if two share one, cut or merge.
- Read it aloud: if three consecutive sentences share a length, vary one.
