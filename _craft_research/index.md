## The prompts behind them

`prompts/` holds [[_genre-comp-template]] — fill its slots (or ask Claude to) to research a
new mode — plus the five prompts kept verbatim: `dialogue-subtext-and-negotiation`,
`endings-and-final-chapter-craft`, `foreshadowing-and-payoff`, `humor-in-dark-frames`, and
`openings-and-cold-open-craft`. The other ten were folded into their reports; ask Claude to
reconstruct one from its report if you want to re-run that topic.
